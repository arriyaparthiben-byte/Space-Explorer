⭐ STARS & STELLAR EVOLUTION

The Lifecycle of Stars

Stars are giant spheres of glowing plasma powered by nuclear fusion in their cores. Depending on their initial mass, stars live for millions to trillions of years before evolving into exotic cosmic remnants.

Protostar & Main Sequence

Collapsing clouds of gas fuse Hydrogen into Helium (like our Sun currently does).

Red Giant Stage

Exhausting core hydrogen causes the outer layers to expand dramatically.

Supernova & Remnants

Massive stars detonate in titanic explosions, leaving White Dwarfs, Neutron Stars, or Black Holes.

🕳️ GRAVITATIONAL MONSTERS

Black Holes & Event Horizons

A black hole is a region of spacetime where gravity is so strong that nothing—no particles or light—can escape from inside its event horizon.

Stellar-Mass

Formed when a massive star collapses at the end of its life (5 to 100 solar masses).

Supermassive

Millions to billions of solar masses residing at the centers of almost all large galaxies (e.g. Sagittarius A*).

🌌 GALAXIES & NEBULAE

Cosmic Islands & Cosmic Nurseries

Galaxies contain hundreds of billions of stars bound together by gravity. Between the stars lie nebulae—vast interstellar clouds of gas and dust where new star systems condense and ignite.

🪐 ALIEN WORLDS

Exoplanets & The Goldilocks Zone

Astronomers have confirmed over 5,500 exoplanets orbiting distant stars. The "Habitable Zone" (Goldilocks Zone) is the orbital distance around a star where liquid water could exist on a planet's surface.
